# Waypoint Racing Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Waypoint Racing account and associated data, send an email to [avimarineinnovations@gmail.com](mailto:avimarineinnovations@gmail.com) with the subject **Account Deletion Request**. Include the email address you used to sign in to the app.

We will send a verification email to that address to confirm your request. Your account and associated app data will be deleted within 30 days of receiving your request, after verification. Deletion is irreversible.

See the [Waypoint Racing Privacy Policy](index.html) for information about the data handled by the app.
